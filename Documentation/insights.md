# Dashboard Insights

This document summarizes the key observations identified from the AWARE AI Digital Wellbeing Analysis Dashboard.

---

## 1. Executive Overview

The Executive Overview provides a high-level view of digital usage, application usage, distraction, and wellbeing indicators.

### Screen Time and Social Media Usage

The dashboard compares average screen time and average social media time across different primary-use categories.

The displayed averages are relatively close across the categories, allowing comparison of digital usage patterns among different primary-use groups.

### Distraction by Keyword

The keyword analysis shows differences in average distraction scores across the selected keywords.

Keywords such as Reels and Gaming appear among the higher-scoring keywords in the displayed analysis, while Interview Preparation appears among the lower-scoring keywords.

### Application Usage

The application analysis highlights the applications contributing the most to recorded usage duration.

This helps identify where digital usage is concentrated.

### Content Category Usage

The dashboard compares usage across content categories including:

- Entertainment
- Social Media
- Sports
- Business
- News
- Education

Entertainment and Social Media represent substantial portions of the recorded usage in the displayed analysis.

# 2. Distraction Intelligence

This page focuses on distraction-related patterns.

## Distraction by Time of Day

The dashboard compares distraction scores across:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Night
- Late Night

The displayed values are relatively close across the different time periods.

This allows comparison of distraction patterns throughout the day.

## Distraction by Burnout Risk

The dashboard compares distraction scores across:

- High
- Medium
- Low

The displayed distribution is relatively balanced across the three burnout-risk groups, with Medium and Low showing slightly higher values than High in the displayed analysis.

## Top Applications by Distraction Score

The application analysis identifies applications with higher recorded distraction scores.

This provides an application-level view of where distraction-related activity is concentrated.

## Distraction by Content Category

The dashboard compares distraction scores across different content categories.

This allows categories with relatively higher recorded distraction scores to be identified.

Average screen time is compared across:

---

# 4. Overall Insights

The dashboard provides a connected view of digital behavior rather than focusing only on total screen time.

The main observations are:

- Digital usage is distributed across multiple applications and content categories.
- Application usage varies across different applications.
- Distraction scores differ across keywords and content categories.
- Distraction scores can be compared across different times of day.
- Distraction-related activity can be viewed alongside burnout-risk groups.
- Screen-time patterns vary across primary-use categories.
- Wellbeing indicators can be compared across user-status groups.
- The dashboard combines digital usage, distraction, sleep, and wellbeing indicators.

---

## Interpretation Note

These observations describe patterns present in the dataset.

They should not be interpreted as proof that a particular application, activity, or digital behavior directly causes changes in wellbeing, sleep, or burnout.
